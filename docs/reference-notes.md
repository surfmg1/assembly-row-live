# Reference notes from the owner's images

Notes only. The images themselves are not stored in the repository. Batch 1 included aerial map screenshots; per the brief's licensing rule they are used here only to correct my understanding of the layout, never as a source for assets or textures. The street photos are the owner's own.

## Batch 1 (10 Nov 2026)

### Layout corrections for `src/sim/city.ts`
- Confirmed: Orange Line and commuter tracks run along the **east** side; Draw Seven Park lies between the tracks and the water; I-93 is west/south-west; the Marketplace strip (Trader Joe's at its north end, Burlington mid-strip, World Market nearby) is on the west with the big lot between it and Grand Union Boulevard.
- Wrong in the current map: the LEGO Discovery Center is **not** in the northernmost block. Northernmost west block is a brick residential courtyard building facing the park. Northernmost east block is an L-shaped white office building with rooftop solar. LEGO is in the **second** block on the west side, a large white-roofed outlet building, with the giraffe ("Gio") at its corner on the cross street.
- Second block east is a dark-roofed residential courtyard building; white residential towers stand further south-east toward the station; Shake Shack sits by the tracks.
- The north end of Assembly Row street splits around a small pavilion building in a tree-lined median plaza before reaching the park. The three rust truss frames stand in a paved plaza right at that street end, not off in the lawn.
- Grand Union Boulevard ends at a **roundabout** by the park, with a small standalone café building at the north end of the lot.
- Missing entirely: an open recreation block in the middle of the district with blue pickleball courts, a lawn and a sand/gravel area (Club Volo), next to the hotel; a long office block with a low curved wing south of it; a marina with finger docks in the river near the rail bridge; a tall rounded glass tower at the south-west near I-93; Home Depot further south-west.
- Backdrop: the casino tower is curved bronze glass on a white arcaded podium with a ferry dock and lawn; a white wind turbine stands across the water to the south-east; the rail bridge is long, low and many-piered; the dam has a small control tower.

### Look and feel
- Lucky Strike Social: black three-storey corner building on Grand Union Boulevard, white capital lettering, red curved arrow sign with bulbs, Mass General Brigham imaging at street level, a white-and-dark gridded glass office tower rising behind it.
- Streets: green-painted bike lanes with white flex posts on rubber curbs, brick-red crosswalks, black fluted lamp posts with acorn lights, blue street-name blades (Revolution Dr), purple "Assembly Marketplace 3 hour parking" signs.
- Buildings: mid-rise residential in orange brick lower floors with grey panel upper floors and deep-set windows; twin towers, one tan and one charcoal; a low pavilion wrapped in a red, white and blue mural; sidewalk cafés with white umbrellas, black metal tables, planters and street trees.
- Winter night reference: heavy snow, plowed banks taller than the bollards, warm sodium glow, the red arrow sign lit.

## Batch 2 (10 Nov 2026)

Owner's instruction: this is a personal, non-commercial project, so the brief's ban on map-screenshot references is lifted. Revisit only if the game is ever published.

### Street plan (from the flat map; river is north, tracks east, I-93 south-west)
- Three north-south streets: **Grand Union Boulevard** (west spine, lot on its west side), **Assembly Row** (middle), and a third street beside the tracks. Grand Union runs from the park roundabout south past Revolution Drive, and from the roundabout it also curves north-west along the river toward Shore Drive and the Route 28 bridge.
- Cross streets, north to south: Great River Road (curving along the park), Canal Street, Artisan Way, Foley Street, Revolution Drive (curves at its east end toward the station). Middlesex Avenue runs behind the Marketplace strip.
- Marketplace strip, north to south: Staples, Trader Joe's, TJ Maxx & HomeGoods, Burlington, World Market. Small standalone café at the north end of the lot.

### Blocks
- Between Grand Union and Assembly Row, north to south: brick courtyard apartments (Earls on the park corner); white outlet building (LEGO, Fuji) with Gio the giraffe at its north-east corner; tan gridded office plus apartment tower (Posto); the open Club Volo block (pickleball, lawn, sand); offices to Revolution Drive.
- East of Assembly Row, north to south: L-shaped white office with the pavilion and tree median in front; dark double-courtyard apartments; **AMC cinema** (white box, sign visible down the street); a garage with a colourful mural facing the tracks; white and tan residential towers; the station headhouse.
- South-east: long Mass General Brigham office with a landscaped garden and a solar-roofed garage along the tracks; Home Depot south of that; hollow-square white apartment block and self-storage toward I-93.

### Water and edges
- Marina with long finger docks at the north-east corner, just upstream of the Orange Line bridge.
- Order going downstream: marina, Orange Line bridge, **dam and locks**, then a second curved commuter-rail bridge. Draw Seven Park is the open field east of the tracks, south of the dam, with a small parking loop.
- Across the river: Gateway Center (Costco, Target) to the north; Encore on its own peninsula to the north-east; wind turbine to the east.
- Boston skyline is due south. Foss Park lies beyond I-93 to the south-west.

### Look and feel
- Dusk reference: warm window light, string-lit trees down Assembly Row, pink sky.
- Snow references: view down to the pavilion and truss frames with the frozen river behind; street view south along Assembly Row with snow-loaded trees and the red AMC sign at the far end; hanging-globe black lamp posts; Earls with a black canopy.
- Masterplan rendering: watercolour style with green roofs, useful as an art-direction option.

## Batch 3 (10 Nov 2026)

- **Truss frames:** three separate rust-brown riveted steel portal frames, each two lattice legs with a cross-braced top, tall enough to walk and drive under, standing in a row on brick paving at the river end of Assembly Row. The middle one carries a "Riverfest" banner. Black acorn-top lamp posts beside them. They are freestanding gateways, not one connected pergola.
- **Park end of the street:** Assembly Row ends in a small turnaround with a curved overlook; riverside paths run both ways from it; the marina's long diagonal dock starts just east of it. Great River Road swings from the roundabout along the north face of the first blocks and on to the track-side street.
- **South-east corner:** Draw Seven Park is a plain open field with a parking loop by the dam. The commuter-rail line splits from the Orange Line south of the station and curves north-east on its own low bridge. The Mass General Brigham garage has a full solar roof and sits right against the tracks; a curving garden lies between it and the long office. Home Depot and its lot fill the block between that and I-93. Industrial yards lie east of the tracks, south of the park.
- **Winter park reference:** deep snow, a plowed dark path curving through bare snow-loaded trees, bright blue sky.

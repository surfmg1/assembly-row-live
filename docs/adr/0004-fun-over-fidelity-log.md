# ADR 0004: Fun-over-fidelity trade-off log

Date: 2026-11-10. Status: living document. The brief asks for each of these to be logged.

| Trade-off | Fidelity would say | The game does | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gravity | 9.8 m/s² | 22 m/s² | Jumps feel snappy instead of floaty |
| Wheels going uphill | Lose all the energy the climb costs | Lose 45% of it | A real 1.4 m kicker eats most of a skater's speed; this one launches you |
| First grind | The player must ollie onto a rail | The first rail sits under the kicker's flight path | Holding forward from the spawn gives roll-in, air and a grind in under 6 seconds |
| Vaults | An animation with a speed cost | Instant, full speed kept, for anything up to 1.25 m | Flow |
| Wall-run | Not physically possible for a second | 1.0 s at 30% gravity, once per jump | The 10 m gap in the parkour run needs it |
| Shopping cart | Unsteerable above a jog | Steerable, with one wobbly wheel that pulls harder with speed | Chaos you can still aim |
| Bail | Injury | A ragdoll, a line of text, and a respawn 1.5 s later | Failure is a joke |
| Hitting a wall on wheels | Depends | Bail above 7 m/s (skate) or 6.5 m/s (cart) into the wall | Fair danger, clearly readable |
